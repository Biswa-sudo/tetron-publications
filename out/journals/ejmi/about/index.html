Aims and Scope

European Journal of Multidisciplinary Insights (EJMI) is a bi-monthly, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and the exchange of knowledge across a wide range of academic fields. The journal aims to publish high-quality, original research that contributes to the advancement of knowledge in both theoretical and applied areas. EJMI is committed to providing fast, transparent, and rigorous peer review to ensure the highest quality of publications. All accepted articles are made freely available online upon publication, promoting global access and the dissemination of knowledge across academic and research communities.

The journal covers a broad spectrum of research areas, including but not limited to the following:

Natural Sciences

Areas such as Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry, Earth Sciences, Environmental Studies, Marine Biology, and Physics. Research in these fields may explore fundamental scientific principles, environmental challenges, and the application of natural science in solving global issues.

Engineering and Technology

Fields such as Aerospace, Agricultural Engineering, Biochemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical and Mechanical Engineering, Nanotechnology, Robotics, and Software Engineering. Research articles may focus on new technologies, engineering advancements, and practical applications in various sectors.

Medical and Health Sciences

Including Basic Medicine, Clinical Medicine, Public Health, Medical Technology, Neurosciences, and Health Informatics. Contributions in this area aim to address current challenges in healthcare, improve patient outcomes, and introduce innovative solutions to medical and health issues.

Agricultural Sciences

This includes research in Agriculture, Animal Science, Agricultural Biotechnology, Crop Science, Forestry, Fisheries, and Veterinary Sciences. Topics may address food security, sustainable agricultural practices, and the role of technology in improving agricultural productivity.

Social Sciences

Covering disciplines such as Anthropology, Economics, Education, Gender Studies, Political Science, Sociology, and Social Work. Research in this field focuses on societal issues, social change, policy impacts, and the role of human behavior in shaping our world.

Humanities

Encompassing subjects like Archaeology, Cultural Studies, Philosophy, Languages, Literature, Religious Studies, and the Arts. Contributions in the humanities explore human culture, history, creativity, and the philosophical foundations of society.

Publication Frequency

European Journal of Multidisciplinary Insights (EJMI) is published bi-monthly, ensuring a continuous flow of high-quality, peer-reviewed research across disciplines.

Publication Ethics and Malpractice

At European Journal of Multidisciplinary Insights (EJMI), we are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of publication ethics. We believe that integrity is essential to the trust and credibility of scientific research, and we uphold this principle in all stages of the publication process. Our editorial practices are guided by the COPE Code of Conduct and Best Practice Guidelines for Journal Editors, as well as the Code of Conduct for Journal Publishers.

To ensure transparency and fairness, we rigorously follow ethical guidelines in our peer-review process, striving to publish only original research free from plagiarism, data manipulation, or any other form of scientific misconduct.

We are proud supporters of the Think. Check. Submit. initiative, which helps researchers identify trustworthy and credible journals. This campaign offers a simple checklist to guide authors in evaluating the legitimacy of journals and publishers before submitting their work.

As part of Tetron Publisher, EJMI takes its responsibility seriously and ensures a professional, ethical approach throughout the entire publishing process. From submission to final publication, we are committed to maintaining the integrity of scholarly communication.

By adhering to these ethical standards, we aim to contribute to a scholarly environment where research is shared with transparency, respect, and accountability.
